## 2026-11-06 - Missing Circle Membership Validation on Pool Operations
**Vulnerability:** In `contracts/kisan circle/src/lib.rs`, functions such as `deposit`, `request_loan`, and `repay_loan` only verified caller authorization (`member.require_auth()`), but did not verify whether the address was actually an enrolled circle member (`join_circle`). Non-member addresses could interact with internal balances and pool accounting without ever being registered in the circle's member list.
**Learning:** Checking signature authentication (`require_auth`) only confirms who signed the transaction, not whether the caller is authorized within the business domain / contract state. In community savings and lending circles, pool interactions must enforce domain-level membership checks.
**Prevention:** Always verify domain registration (e.g., membership existence in persistent storage) alongside identity authentication for any state-mutating actions on pooled cooperative contracts.
